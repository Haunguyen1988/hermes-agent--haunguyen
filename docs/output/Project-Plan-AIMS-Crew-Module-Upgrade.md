## 1. Objective

Upgrade and optimize the AIMS Crew Management module to:
- close identified operational and compliance gaps (12 gaps from FGA-AIMS-CRW-001)
- reduce manual effort in crew pairing, rostering, and notification
- ensure full regulatory compliance for FTL calculations
- modernize crew communication through portal activation
- establish standardized governance for crew-related access and processes

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope
- Crew pairing rule configuration and optimization
- Crew rostering process improvement and change logging
- Crew portal activation (roster publication, swap/trade, notifications)
- Crew qualification and currency alert configuration
- FTL calculation compliance with local CAA regulations
- Crew management reporting improvement
- Supporting SOPs and governance documents

### Out of Scope (Phase 2 / Deferred)
- Training system integration (evaluated separately)
- Management BI dashboard (after KPI definition)
- Payroll integration
- Crew mobile app (beyond portal)
