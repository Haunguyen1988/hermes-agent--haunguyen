## 1. Summary

An internal fit-gap assessment of the AIMS Crew Management module has identified **12 gaps** across 5 functional areas (crew pairing, rostering, qualification tracking, FTL monitoring, and reporting). Of these, **5 are critical** — directly affecting crew scheduling quality, regulatory compliance, and operational communication.

The current configuration relies on significant manual effort and workarounds, creating operational risk and inefficiency. A structured upgrade is recommended to address the most impactful gaps while maintaining operational stability.

---

## 2. Recommendation

**Proceed with a phased AIMS Crew Management upgrade**, prioritizing:

1. **Pairing rule configuration** — to reduce manual adjustment and compliance risk
2. **FTL regulatory compliance** — to eliminate regulatory exposure from incomplete local rule configuration
3. **Crew portal activation** — to modernize roster communication and enable crew self-service

These three items address the highest-impact gaps and are feasible within the current license and vendor engagement framework.

---

## 3. Rationale

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Operational efficiency** | Current manual pairing adjustments consume significant Crew Planning time. Proper rule configuration will reduce rework by an estimated 40-60%. |
| **Regulatory compliance** | FTL calculation gaps create exposure to local CAA non-compliance. Addressing this is mandatory, not optional. |
| **Crew satisfaction** | Delayed roster communication and lack of self-service increase crew complaints and manual follow-up workload. |
| **Cost avoidance** | Suboptimal deadhead management and manual processes represent ongoing hidden costs. |
| **Implementation readiness** | Most critical items are configuration-level changes, not system development — reducing timeline and risk. |
