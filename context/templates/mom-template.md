# MOM Template

Use this template when preparing Minutes of Meeting (MOM), meeting recap, workshop summary, or post-discussion action record for Hau.

The purpose of this template is to capture discussion points, decisions, open items, and agreed actions in a format that is easy to send and easy to follow up.

---

# [Meeting Title]

## 1. Meeting Information
- Date:
- Time:
- Location / Platform:
- Meeting Type:
- Facilitator / PIC:

## 2. Participants
List participants or groups involved.
Where helpful, include department or role.

## 3. Purpose Of Meeting
State briefly why the meeting was held.
Keep this short and practical.

## 4. Key Discussion Points
Summarize the main topics discussed.
Write them as structured points, not as a full transcript.

Recommended style:
### 4.1 [Topic]
- key point
- clarification given
- issue raised
- current understanding

### 4.2 [Topic]
- key point
- clarification given
- issue raised
- current understanding

## 5. Decisions / Agreements
Capture what was agreed, confirmed, or aligned.
Separate confirmed decisions from ideas or suggestions.

Examples:
- approach confirmed
- owner alignment agreed
- vendor to provide clarification
- department to validate process flow
- timeline tentatively accepted

## 6. Open Issues / Pending Clarifications
List items that remain unresolved.
Make it clear what still requires:
- vendor clarification
- internal decision
- data confirmation
- process validation
- management approval

## 7. Action Items
Capture agreed actions in a follow-up oriented format.

Recommended table logic:
- ID
- Action Item
- Owner
- Support / Stakeholder
- Due Date
- Status / Note

## 8. Risks / Concerns
Capture key concerns that may affect timeline, scope, process readiness, system logic, or operational implementation.
This section should be concise and focused on follow-up relevance.

## 9. Next Meeting / Next Step
State the next planned checkpoint, meeting, or immediate follow-up action if known.

---

## Short MOM Mode

When the user needs a compact version, use this sequence:
- Purpose
- Key Points
- Decisions
- Open Issues
- Action Items

## Writing Rules For This Template

When using this MOM template:
- do not write a verbatim transcript
- summarize discussion into usable points
- make decisions explicit
- make open items visible
- make owners and next steps easy to track
- keep the tone professional and neutral
- write in a way that can be sent immediately after the meeting

## Quality Standard

A good MOM should help Hau immediately:
- send the meeting recap
- align participants on what was discussed
- track agreed actions
- identify what is still open
- reduce ambiguity after the meeting
