# Example — Responsibilities of Related Departments

## Use Case
Draft a formal section defining responsibilities of operational departments in relation to AIMS or another operational system.

## Example Prompt
Write a formal internal document section titled "Responsibilities of Related Operational Departments" for an airline operational systems SOP.
Use numbered clauses and clear accountability language.

## Example Output

# Responsibilities of Related Operational Departments

## 1. OCC
OCC shall:
- coordinate operational use of system outputs relevant to daily flight operations
- report issues affecting operational continuity
- support validation of workflow practicality during implementation or change

## 2. Crew Department
The Crew Department shall:
- confirm business requirements for crew-related workflow and access
- validate usability of relevant system functions
- support testing and review where crew process changes are involved

## 3. Maintenance
Maintenance shall:
- provide process requirements for maintenance-related workflow
- report any system issue affecting maintenance operational control or visibility
- support clarification of technical data usage where relevant

## 4. Ground Operations
Ground Operations shall:
- validate ground process practicality where system workflow affects airport execution
- provide input on handoff timing and operational dependency
- report disruption or usability concerns affecting execution

## 5. ICT
ICT shall:
- support technical administration and troubleshooting
- perform approved system configuration or user administration actions where assigned
- coordinate with vendor on technical issues beyond internal resolution
